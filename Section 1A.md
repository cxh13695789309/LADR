### 1.3 Properties of Complex Arithmetic
#### commutativity
>$\alpha+\beta=\beta+\alpha,\alpha\beta=\beta\alpha.$ for all $\alpha,\beta \in \mathbf C$

#### associativity
>$(\alpha+\beta)+\lambda=\alpha+(\beta+\lambda)$ and $(\alpha\beta)\lambda=\alpha(\beta\lambda)$ for all $\alpha,\beta,\lambda \in \mathbf C$.

#### multiplicative inverse  

>For every $\alpha\in\mathbf C$ with $\alpha\neq0$, there exists a unique $\beta\in\mathbf C$ such that
>$$
>\alpha\beta=1.
>$$
This unique number is denoted by 
>$$\beta=\alpha^{-1}=\frac1\alpha.$$
